Data sources

Some answers are estimates and may be imprecise. Where a source had no figure for a country, or none that could be checked, the value is an estimate. If an answer looks wrong, the game lets the players overrule the check during play.

The code is under the MIT licence. The data comes from the third-party sources below. The same list, with more detail, is in DATA-LICENSES.md in the project repository.

mledoze/countries
Used for: country names, ISO codes, capital, currency, native name, area, internet domain, land borders and region. Licence: Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0. The combined country database in data/countries.json is a derived database and is shared under the same licence. Source: github.com/mledoze/countries
World Bank World Development Indicators
Used for: population (SP.POP.TOTL), GDP per capita (NY.GDP.PCAP.CD), life expectancy (SP.DYN.LE00.IN) and homicide rate (VC.IHR.PSRC.P5). Licence: CC BY 4.0. Source: data.worldbank.org
Our World in Data and NCD-RisC
Used for: average male height (NCD-RisC, 1996 birth cohort, via Our World in Data) and a few homicide rates (UNODC data via Our World in Data). Licence: CC BY 4.0. Source: ourworldindata.org
UNODC
Used for: homicide rates the World Bank does not list, via Our World in Data. United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime, dataunodc.un.org.
Natural Earth
Used for: country outlines and shapes (simplified), via the datasets/geo-countries repository. Licence: public domain. Source: naturalearthdata.com
Global Firepower 2026
Used for: military ranking. The rankings are credited to globalfirepower.com and reproduced for a non-commercial quiz. Countries it does not rank have an estimated place.
Wikipedia
Used for: some text facts such as national sports, highest points, national animals, dishes, landmarks and alternative names. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. Source: en.wikipedia.org
Other public sources
Pew Research Center (religion), the WHO Global status report on alcohol and health (drink shares, via Wikipedia), the Observatory of Economic Complexity (main exports), UNESCO (landmarks) and national statistics offices. The source of each value is recorded in the sources field of data/countries.json.
Wikimedia Commons photos
Used for: portraits, dishes, landmarks and drinks shown after an answer. Each photo keeps its own licence, author and file page, listed on the image credits page.
