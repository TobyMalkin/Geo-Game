Who is playing?

Take turns on one screen. On your turn, spin the category wheel, then spin the country wheel, then answer the question.

  • A right answer wins you that country. Nobody else can win it afterwards.
  • Right or wrong, that category is used up for you.
  • The game ends when everyone votes to end (checked at the end of a round), when nobody has categories left, or when every country is won.
  • Most countries wins.
